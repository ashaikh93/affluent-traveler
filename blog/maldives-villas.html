The 5 Best Overwater Villas in the Maldives (2026)

By The Affluent Traveler · 8 min read · Updated November 2026

There are lagoons, and then there are lagoons you can step into from your own bedroom. In the Maldives, the overwater villa is the ultimate address.

The country’s finest resorts now compete on privacy, design and the quality of personal service. A suspended terrace, an infinity pool of your own and a butler who anticipates your every request have become the benchmark for an exceptional stay. After weighing design, seclusion, service and sheer sense of occasion, these are the five overwater villas we would book in 2026.

01Soneva Jani

Atoll NoonuTransfer Seaplane, approx. 40 minFrom approx. $2,500 per night

Soneva Jani redefines the genre. Its Water Retreats sit above a vast, impossibly clear lagoon, and many feature a retractable roof above the bed, so you can fall asleep beneath the stars. Each villa has its own pool, and the larger retreats add a water slide that drops straight into the sea, a flourish that is playful without ever feeling frivolous.

Service is intuitive rather than intrusive. Your dedicated host handles everything from private dining on a sandbank to the arrangement of an in-villa cinema evening. Expect nightly rates from roughly $2,500, rising well beyond $10,000 for multi-bedroom retreats.

02Cheval Blanc Randheli

Atoll NoonuTransfer Seaplane, approx. 45 minFrom approx. $3,000 per night

For quiet French elegance, nothing in the archipelago compares. Cheval Blanc Randheli treats the Maldives like a private estate, with tailored interiors, bespoke touches and a hush that settles over the island by early evening. The Water Villas come with private pools and sun decks, and the look is understated: pale woods, soft linens and a restrained palette that lets the ocean take centre stage.

The service model is highly personalised, with a dedicated host assigned to each villa. This is the choice for travellers who prize discretion above spectacle. Rates start around $3,000 per night and climb steeply in peak season.

03One&Only Reethi Rah

Atoll North MaléTransfer Speedboat, approx. 45 minFrom approx. $1,800 per night

Reethi Rah has long been a favourite of those who know the Maldives well. The island is generously proportioned, which means villas are spaced apart, and the Water Villas, many with private pools, feel genuinely secluded. A speedboat transfer from Malé keeps arrival effortless, with no seaplane schedule to plan around.

Villa butlers manage the details, from stocking your terrace with your preferred drinks to booking the island’s restaurants and spa. For the polish of a world-class brand paired with true tropical privacy, it is hard to fault. Expect roughly $1,800 to $6,000 per night depending on villa category and season.

04Gili Lankanfushi

Atoll North MaléTransfer Speedboat, approx. 20 minFrom approx. $1,500 per night

Barefoot luxury at its most refined. Gili Lankanfushi’s thatched villas stand on stilts above the lagoon, each with its own private “Mr or Ms Friday” butler, who moves between your villa and the island by bicycle or boat. Several categories include private pools, and the generous decks, hammocks and direct lagoon access make it easy to forget there is anywhere else to be.

Its closeness to Malé means you can be in your villa within the hour. Rates begin near $1,500 per night, making it the most accessible entry on this list without sacrificing a sense of occasion.

05The St. Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort

Atoll DhaaluTransfer Seaplane, approx. 35 minFrom approx. $1,800 per night

Bold, contemporary and unapologetically glamorous, The St. Regis brings its signature butler service to the lagoon. Overwater villas here include private pools and sweeping ocean views, and the resort’s dining and sundowner rituals are among the most polished in the country.

The tone is lively but never crowded, which suits couples and families seeking indulgence with energy. Nightly rates typically fall between $1,800 and $5,000, depending on villa type and travel dates.

Planning Your Stay

The best weather runs from December to April, when skies are clear and seas calm, though this is also when prices peak. The shoulder months of November and May often offer superb value, along with fewer guests. If you travel in the wetter season, expect short showers and noticeably lower rates.

Book early for holiday periods, and ask about transfers when you reserve. Seaplane flights operate in daylight only, so a late arrival may require a night in Malé. Many resorts also offer perks for longer stays, including complimentary upgrades, dining credits and spa treatments, so it is always worth asking.
