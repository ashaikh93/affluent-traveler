Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

At The Affluent Traveler (“we”, “us” or “our”), we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit theaffluenttraveler.online (the “Site”), how we use it, and the choices you have.

In short: we do not sell your personal data. We collect only what we need to run the Site, understand how it is used, and keep it secure.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

We collect personal information only when you choose to give it to us, for example when you:

  • Contact us by email or through a contact form (your name, email address and message).
  • Subscribe to our newsletter, if offered (your email address).
  • Leave a comment on an article, if comments are enabled.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the Site, certain technical information is collected automatically, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring website, pages viewed and the date and time of your visit.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate, maintain and improve the Site and our content.
  • To respond to your enquiries and messages.
  • To send newsletters or updates you have asked to receive (you may unsubscribe at any time).
  • To analyze traffic and understand which content readers value most.
  • To detect, prevent and address technical issues, abuse or security threats.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to third parties. We share information only in the limited circumstances described in this policy, such as with service providers who help us run the Site, or where required by law.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use basic cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Make the Site work: essential cookies that support core functionality and security.
  • Understand usage: analytics cookies that help us see how visitors use the Site, in aggregate form.
  • Track affiliate referrals: cookies set by our travel partners when you click an affiliate link (see Section 5).

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain features of the Site. Where required by law, we will ask for your consent before setting non-essential cookies.

5. Affiliate Links and Third-Party Websites

Our articles may contain affiliate links to partners such as Booking.com and Agoda. When you click one of these links, you leave our Site and the partner’s own privacy policy and cookie practices apply. Partners may place cookies on your device so that a booking can be attributed to us. We do not receive your booking details or payment information.

For more information, please read our Affiliate Disclosure. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of third-party websites, and we encourage you to review their policies.

6. Service Providers

We may use trusted third parties for services such as website hosting, analytics, email delivery and security. These providers may process information on our behalf and are permitted to use it only to perform those services.

7. Data Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required by law. For example, we keep newsletter subscriber details until you unsubscribe, and correspondence for as long as needed to handle your enquiry.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and California), you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your information.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.
  • Request a portable copy of your data.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable time and as required by applicable law.

9. Children’s Privacy

The Site is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. International Visitors

Our readers are located around the world. Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, which may have different data protection laws. By using the Site, you understand that your information may be transferred and processed accordingly.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review it periodically.
