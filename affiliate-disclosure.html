Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: November 5, 2026

The Affluent Traveler is committed to being open with our readers. This page explains how we earn revenue so you can make informed decisions when using our recommendations.

The short version: The Affluent Traveler participates in affiliate programs, including those of Booking.com and Agoda. If you click an affiliate link on our Site and make a booking, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

How It Works

1

You click a link in one of our articles.

2

You book on the partner’s website at the same price you would normally pay.

3

The partner may pay us a small commission for the referral.

What Are Affiliate Links?

An affiliate link is a special tracking link that lets a travel company know you came from our Site. If you click the link and complete a qualifying booking or purchase, we may receive a commission from the company. The price you pay is the same whether you use our link or go to the partner directly.

Our Affiliate Partners

We currently participate in affiliate programs with travel booking platforms, including:

Booking.com

Hotels, resorts, villas and other accommodation worldwide.

Agoda

Hotels and stays, with particular strength across Asia and the Middle East.

We may add or change partners over time, and may also work with other travel-related companies. This disclosure applies to every affiliate link on the Site.

How This Supports Our Work

Commissions help cover the costs of running The Affluent Traveler, including research, writing, photography, hosting and site maintenance. They allow us to keep our content free for readers.

Editorial Independence

Our editorial opinions are our own. We choose which destinations, hotels and experiences to feature based on quality, relevance and value to our readers, not on the commission offered. Affiliate relationships do not influence our reviews, rankings or recommendations. If an article is sponsored or features a complimentary stay, we will say so clearly.

Prices, Availability and Accuracy

We work hard to keep our content accurate, but prices, availability, offers and policies change frequently. Please confirm all details on the partner’s website before you book. We are not a party to any transaction between you and a third-party provider, and we are not responsible for bookings, cancellations or services provided by those companies.

Cookies and Tracking

To credit us for a referral, our partners may place cookies on your device when you click an affiliate link. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. For more detail, please see our Privacy Policy.

Legal Compliance

This disclosure is provided in line with consumer protection and advertising guidelines, including the U.S. Federal Trade Commission’s guidance on endorsements and the requirements of similar regulators worldwide.
